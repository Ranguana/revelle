**Tagline:** Sunday since yesterday.

**Premise:** The mole has been going since yesterday and everyone knows it —
that is the whole schedule, and nobody has ever written it down. The courtyard
door stands open from noon; nobody greets you, you are seated, and a plate
arrives before your name does. The comida starts around two and is still the
afternoon at six: three generations talking at once down a table that grows
chairs as cousins of cousins come through the door. Nothing is performed and
nothing is announced — the food is the only thing presented, and it is presented
like an argument won years ago. At dusk it dissolves into the courtyard, chairs
pulled to the wall, café de olla, the same stories retold with the same
corrections, and nobody calls it an ending because Sunday does not end, it
recedes.

**Speaker:** The house itself, in its standing authority — the voice of a
kitchen that has done this every Sunday since before you were born and will not
be explaining itself now.

**Address:** Familial imperative. *Come. Sit. Eat first.* You are not asked; you
are included.

**Cadence:** Unhurried, abundant, circling — the rhythm of something that
repeats on purpose. Sentences that refill your plate.

**Formality:** Plain.

**Humour:** Fond — the stories are at the whole table's expense and the whole
table has heard them, and the corrections are part of the telling.

**Mechanism:** *Nothing is announced, because nothing needs to be — the hour is
in everybody's bones.* The house does not schedule; it remembers.

**Reply convention** (audience evidence — semi): *Nobody is invited. The door is
open and Sunday is known.*

**Lexicon:** the comida · the mole, since yesterday · the courtyard · the long
table · more chairs · mezcal in clay · café de olla · the pot that never empties ·
dusk against the wall

**Tone tags (proposed, 7):** `lingers`, `good_natured`, `nicknames`, `in_jokes`,
`sentimental`, `teasing`, `compliments_plainly`
— all real; four are Catskills' (`nicknames`, `in_jokes`, `sentimental`,
`teasing`), which is why that pair reaches 0.876.

## Twin separation vs Aspen 1994

Aspen is fast, adrenal, obedient to the mountain's posted clock, stops dead for
powder. Oaxaca is unhurried, generational, obedient to nothing but custom, and
recedes rather than stops. Cadence and earnestness run opposite; the two should
sit at the far ends of the pace axis. **If this voice speeds up, it has been
written wrong.**

NOTE: this separation is asserted and cannot yet be measured. Aspen has no voice
and no tone tags.
