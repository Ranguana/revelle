# ACAPULCO, 1959 — founder draft

**DRAFT. Not integrated.** See VERIFICATION.md — the tone tags breach the 0.65
voice ceiling against Havana and against Oaxaca. The prose below is untouched.

**Tagline:** The divers go at dusk, and the night follows them down.

**Premise:** A white villa above the bay, and no door to arrive at — the terrace
absorbs you, hands you something cold, and turns you toward the water. The
divers go at dusk: the one fixed thing in the whole night, and the terrace
watches in white jackets with drinks held still. Dinner follows them — platters
up from town, nobody in this house has seen the kitchen — and then the band, and
then the pool lit up, and the night runs until the fishing boats go out and
somebody says so from the railing, fondly, like a toast. It is loud in three
conversations and one language of delight.

**Speaker:** The host somewhere in the crowd, pointing things out — written as
someone *showing you*, certain you have never seen anything like this, correct.

**Address:** Second person, direct, demonstrative. *Look.* The voice takes your
elbow and turns you toward things.

**Cadence:** Warm, sweeping, unhurried but never quiet. Sentences that open
their arms.

**Formality:** Cordial — the room is dressed, but the warmth outranks the jackets.

**Humour:** Delighted. The joke is pleasure itself, and it is on nobody.

**Mechanism:** *Nothing here is ironic. The beauty gets said out loud, and
saying it is the point.* Where a colder house would murmur, this one declares —
the dive is magnificent and the voice says magnificent. Wonder is stated, never
performed at a remove.

**Reply convention** (audience evidence — semi): *Somebody always brings
somebody; the table is set long on purpose.*

**Lexicon:** the terrace · the dive · white jackets · the band · the bay lit up ·
platters up from town · the pool at midnight · the last song · the boats going out

**Tone tags (proposed, 7):** `says_it_out_loud`, `good_natured`, `dressed_up`,
`across_the_room`, `no_dead_air`, `laughs_first`, `lingers`
— all real; four are Havana's, which is why the pair breaches.

## Twin separation vs St. Moritz 1984

St. Moritz is dry, knowing, apology-champagne, teased-back-into-your-coat —
wonder at a remove. Acapulco's mechanism is the inversion: earnest, declarative,
delighted. Theatricality runs positive here and negative nowhere; knowingness
runs negative. **If a correction pass makes this voice arch, it converges on its
twin and un-admits itself.**

NOTE: this separation is asserted and cannot yet be measured. St. Moritz has no
voice and no tone tags.
